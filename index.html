Welcome to the Yuqian (Sophia) Yang Research Page

I am a materials research scientist specializing in semiconductor technologies[cite: 13]. By integrating core principles from chemistry, physics, and materials science, I design advanced semiconductor materials, spintronic systems, and next-generation energy devices[cite: 13].

My core experimental expertise centers on photovoltaics and spintronics, with extensive experience in scalable thin-film deposition, buried interface chemistry, and data-driven process optimization. By coupling automated experimental workflows with machine learning frameworks, my research bridges molecular-level design with device-level performance[cite: 14].

I have a proven track record of translating lab-scale mechanistic insights into high-performance, stable devices and module-scale systems. Looking ahead, I aim to advance autonomous self-driving laboratories and high-throughput materials exploration for next-generation clean energy and quantum information technologies[cite: 14].
